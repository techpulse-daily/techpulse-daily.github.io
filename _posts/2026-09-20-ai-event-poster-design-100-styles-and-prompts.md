The author prompted ChatGPT with event details and repeatedly asked for poster designs in different visual styles, demonstrating that AI can produce a wide variety of non‑generic poster aesthetics.

## Why it matters

It shows that developers can guide generative AI to create distinctive, editable design assets rather than default, repetitive outputs. This flexibility reduces the need for manual redesign and speeds up content creation for marketing tools. It also highlights that AI image models can output layered, editable formats (HTML, PNG, PDF) suitable for integration into software workflows.

## The key fact

The author created a catalogue of one hundred poster styles with ready‑to‑paste prompts and example images for each one.

## Context

AI‑generated event posters have become notorious for their identical, pastel‑heavy look, prompting creators to explore more varied design prompts.
