The EU introduced the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) that will apply from 12 August 2026, requiring producers to register and meet packaging waste obligations in each Member State where their packaging becomes waste. The new rules impose separate registration, reporting, and authorised‑representative costs for micro‑entrepreneurs selling across the EU.

## Why it matters

Micro‑makers and micro‑entrepreneurs must bear hundreds of euros in annual compliance fees per country, making cross‑border sales financially unviable. The administrative burden diverts time and resources away from product development and innovation. Consequently, many small hardware creators may cease EU sales, reducing the availability of niche, open‑source hardware and related services.

## The key fact

A Greek engineer selling only a few boards would face an optimistic total cost of about €1,150 per year to comply with packaging obligations in just four EU countries.

## Context

The PPWR expands existing Extended Producer Responsibility schemes but retains a fragmented national model, forcing each seller to meet separate national requirements rather than a single EU‑wide system.
