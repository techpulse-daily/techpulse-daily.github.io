Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1, two versions of the same model with different safeguard levels. Fable 5.1 is generally available, while Mythos 5.1 is limited to trusted access programs for cybersecurity and life‑science work.

## Why it matters

The models offer higher coding and knowledge‑work performance than the prior Fable 5, reducing cost for typical workloads by about 25% and up to 45% for highly agentic tasks. New safeguards cut false‑positive security flags by 60%, improving usability for security professionals. Enterprise Frontier Safeguards provide zero‑data‑retention privacy, addressing customer concerns about data handling.

## The key fact

Fable 5.1 reduces false‑positive cybersecurity safeguards by 60% compared to earlier versions.

## Context

This launch follows Anthropic’s incremental upgrades to its Claude series, aiming to combine stronger AI capabilities with lower pricing and stronger privacy safeguards for enterprise users.
