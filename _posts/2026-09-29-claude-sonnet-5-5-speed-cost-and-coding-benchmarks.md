Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5.5, the second model in the Claude 5.5 family, as a faster, lower‑cost complement to Claude Opus 5.5. The new model runs over 30% faster and typically costs up to 30% less per task than its predecessor Sonnet 5.

## Why it matters

It can complete well‑scoped coding, document, slide, and spreadsheet tasks with fewer tokens, reducing compute expense for developers. Its faster generation and lower token usage enable quicker iteration in agile workflows. The model’s improved accuracy and reduced tool calls lower the risk of failed builds and support tickets, speeding up software delivery and support operations.

## The key fact

Sonnet 5.5 scores 70.6% on the Terminal‑Bench 4.0 agentic coding benchmark, compared to Sonnet 5’s 10.3% and only two points below Opus 5.5.

## Context

Sonnet 5.5 follows the original Sonnet 5 model, offering a mid‑tier, high‑throughput option in Anthropic’s lineup positioned between the lower‑cost Haiku 5.5 and the higher‑capability Opus 5.5.
