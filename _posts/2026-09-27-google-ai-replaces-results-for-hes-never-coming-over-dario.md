The author searched the phrase "hes never coming over dario" on Google and received an AI-generated overview instead of the expected links to old tweets and Reddit posts.

## Why it matters

Engineers must account for AI-generated answer overlays that can replace traditional search results, potentially altering user expectations and task completion. This shift can affect click-through rates and the relevance of organic content. It also raises design challenges around when to present AI answers versus standard listings.

The article was published on 27 Sep 2026.

## Context

The search query referenced a 2014 fan meme about Philadelphia 76ers draft pick Dario Saric, who was said to be "never coming over" from Turkey.
