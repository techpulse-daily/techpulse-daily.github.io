LibreOffice 26.8 was released on August 26 and in its first week the installer was downloaded over 1 million times. The Document Foundation then published a post stating that LibreOffice intentionally does not include built‑in generative AI features.

## Why it matters

Engineers see a major office suite gaining popularity without AI, indicating demand for privacy‑focused tools. Users gain a free, AI‑free alternative that guarantees no data leaves their computer. Developers can rely on community plugins for AI integration rather than core code changes.

## The key fact

In one week, the LibreOffice 26.8 installer was downloaded more than 1 million times.

## Context

LibreOffice, a free office suite around for nearly two decades, has historically added features but chose to omit built‑in AI in its latest major release.
