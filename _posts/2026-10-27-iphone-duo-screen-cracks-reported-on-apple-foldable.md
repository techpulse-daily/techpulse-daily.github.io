Owners of Apple's new iPhone Duo reported cracks appearing on the inner screens of the foldable handset shortly after release. Apple confirmed that a small number of units have been affected by a display glitch and advised users to contact AppleCare for a replacement.

## Why it matters

Hardware display failures on foldable devices can disrupt app layouts, UI rendering, and touch responsiveness for affected users. Software engineers must account for physical hinge constraints and unexpected hardware failures when designing user interfaces for foldable hardware. Fast and transparent customer replacement policies help contain device category damage during early hardware rollouts.

## The key fact

The iPhone Duo retails starting from $1,999 (£1,499).

## Context

This issue follows similar screen problems experienced by Samsung with its original Galaxy Fold in 2019 during early product rollouts.
