The author observed hundreds to thousands of software bugs each week and wrote a post describing the phenomenon of "bug blindness" where most people miss these issues.

## Why it matters

Engineers who are blind to bugs may ship products with severe usability problems. Recognizing bug blindness helps teams proactively identify and fix hidden defects. It also encourages a culture of critical evaluation rather than assuming software works as intended.

## The key fact

"I easily observe hundreds to thousands of bugs per week"

## Context

Bug blindness refers to the tendency for users and even developers to overlook recurring software defects, a topic the author explores through personal examples and industry anecdotes.
