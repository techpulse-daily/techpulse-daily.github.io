OpenAI announced the release of two new GPT‑6 models, Sol and Luna, as part of the GPT‑6 family. The models are now available in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API (gpt-6-sol, gpt-6-luna).

## Why it matters

Software engineers can access higher‑performing language models at roughly half the cost of the prior GPT‑5.6 pricing, enabling more extensive experimentation and iteration. The lower token prices and improved caching reduce both runtime expenses and latency for AI‑driven coding tools and agents. Faster, cheaper inference also makes it feasible to embed advanced AI features into everyday applications and larger‑scale workflows.

## The key fact

API pricing for GPT‑6 Sol dropped from $4 to $2 per 1 million input tokens (and from $20 to $10 per 1 million output tokens), a 50% reduction compared with GPT‑5.6 promotional pricing.

## Context

The launch follows OpenAI’s earlier release of GPT‑6 Astra, which introduced a new generation of high‑quality, aligned models, prompting a rollout of more cost‑efficient variants for broader use.
