OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Sol, an upgrade to GPT-6 Sol that approaches GPT-6 Astra's intelligence on agentic coding, computer use, and professional tasks. The model is available via the API as gpt-6.1-sol and to paid tiers in ChatGPT Work and Codex at one-fifth of Astra's standard token pricing.

## Why it matters

Cached input costs drop to $0.10 per million tokens, significantly lowering the expense of running agentic systems that repeatedly reuse context across requests. Software engineers can match top-tier Astra performance on long-horizon software engineering tasks at roughly one-fifth of the previous cost. The model also offers higher accuracy and lower error rates across multi-step business workflows and computer-use tasks without requiring expensive frontier-tier compute.

## The key fact

On the DeepSWE v1.1 software-engineering benchmark, GPT-6.1 Sol matches GPT-6 Astra at roughly one-fifth of the cost while outperforming GPT-6 Sol's best score by 6.4 percentage points.

## Context

The update builds directly on OpenAI's GPT-6 model family introduced in September 2026, aiming to close the capability gap between high-end frontier models and cheaper everyday production tiers.
