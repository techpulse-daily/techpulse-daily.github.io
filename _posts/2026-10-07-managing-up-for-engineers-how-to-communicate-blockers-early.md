Telling your boss about a missed deadline on Friday morning is an emergency, but telling them on Monday is just a helpful heads-up. Timing changes everything in engineering execution.

## The Cost of Silence

Managing up means zero surprises. This is the deliberate practice of keeping your leadership informed before problems turn into fires. When a database query slows down your migration, your instinct is often to stay quiet and fix it yourself. You spend twenty hours wrestling with an index before anyone else knows there is a delay.

That silence creates the emergency because your director cannot adjust the release schedule without warning.

## Shifting the Timeline

Instead, you send a note the moment a blocker appears, shifting the timeline early when adjustments are still cheap.
