F-Droid released version 2.0, a complete redesign of the official app after more than a year of development.

## Why it matters

The new UI follows modern Android Material Design, making it easier for engineers to integrate and extend the app. Improved discovery, search, and filtering help developers reach users more effectively. The unified installer and automatic background updates simplify distribution and maintenance of open‑source Android apps.

## The key fact

"F-Droid 2.0 ... the largest app update in 10 years"

## Context

F-Droid has been the primary catalog for free and open‑source Android applications for over a decade, and this major overhaul updates its architecture to current Android standards.
