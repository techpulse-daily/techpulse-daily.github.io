Anthropic launched Claude Haiku 5.5, its newest small language model, and made it generally available on all cloud platforms and the Claude Platform. The release also includes new pricing that lowers Haiku 5.5’s cost compared with Haiku 4.5 and halves the price of Sonnet 5.5 cache reads.

## Why it matters

Engineers can run high‑volume, cost‑sensitive tasks such as summarization, classification, and database queries at a much lower price point. The model’s speed improvements—up to 2.5× faster inference per agent turn—make it suitable for latency‑critical use cases like live customer support and browser automation. Its adjustable effort setting lets developers balance cost against intelligence for each request.

## The key fact

Claude Haiku 5.5 runs about 75% cheaper on average than Claude Haiku 4.5, and for prompts up to 100 k tokens the price per 1 M input tokens is $0.10 versus $0.10/$0.50 for Haiku 4.5.

## Context

Haiku‑class models are Anthropic’s smaller, faster offerings designed for large‑scale agentic workloads, and Haiku 5.5 is the first in the line to add an adjustable effort setting and significant cost reductions.
