DuckLabs announced that it will join Amazon Web Services (AWS), with the acquisition expected to take effect in early September. The team will remain based in Amsterdam and continue working on DuckDB, DuckLake, and Quack.

## Why it matters

DuckDB and related tools will remain open source under the MIT license and governed by the nonprofit DuckDB Foundation. Developers will gain an open extension stack allowing third-party signed extensions to run directly in DuckDB. AWS and DuckLabs plan to use the technology to power new data services across cloud infrastructure.

## The key fact

DuckDB currently sees more than one million downloads every day.

## Context

DuckLabs was founded a little over five years ago as a bootstrapped spinout from CWI and collaborated with AWS for over a year prior to the acquisition.
