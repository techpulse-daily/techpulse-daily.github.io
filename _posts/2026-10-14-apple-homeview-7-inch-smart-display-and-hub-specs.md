Apple announced the Apple HomeView, a smart home device and central hub featuring a 7-inch Liquid Retina display, Siri AI, and a 12MP Center Stage camera. It is available for pre-order starting November 13 in four colors and offers both tabletop and wall-mounted options.

## Why it matters

It provides multi-user support recognizing up to six people to deliver personalized audio, visuals, and AI-driven assistance using Apple Intelligence. The device integrates deeply with the Home app, supporting smart home accessories, multiroom audio via AirPlay, and smoke or carbon monoxide detector sound alerts.

## The key fact

The 7-inch Liquid Retina display features a brightness range from 500 nits for sunny rooms down to 0.3 nits for bedside use.
