Independent web development educators, course creators, and technical writers have reported a severe collapse in their business revenues and website traffic. Many are shutting down their training projects, taking their blogs and books offline, or leaving public-facing developer roles due to burnout and the impact of generative AI.

## Why it matters

The shift toward error-prone, nondeterministic chatbots and LLMs for coding education eliminates the economic incentive to produce high-quality free content and structured curriculums. Additionally, technical editors now face increased workloads and subtle inaccuracies introduced when writers rely on AI tools to save time. This dynamic erodes the foundational human expertise, mentorship, and accessible knowledge required to train future web developers.

## The key fact

Axel Rauschmayer stated that the income from his book sales went from being enough for him to live off in 2024 to zero in 2026.

## Context

Generative AI crawlers and predictive algorithms have increasingly scraped online educational materials without consent or compensation, siphoning web traffic away from human creators and replacing authentic human collaboration with automated chatbots.
