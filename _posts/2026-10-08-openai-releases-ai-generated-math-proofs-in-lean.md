OpenAI is releasing a broad range of new mathematical results produced by an internal frontier model. The results are being published in a GitHub repository along with formalizations of proofs in the Lean programming language.

## Why it matters

Software engineers and scientists can utilize these formalizations, which allow mathematical proofs to be checked by a computer. They can also review additional details in the repository, such as summaries of the model's reasoning and compute estimates. Furthermore, the funding of upcoming workshops and conferences will help researchers understand major AI-produced results.

## The key fact

The average result used the equivalent compute of roughly three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking.

## Context

To develop best practices for sharing these results, OpenAI has been consulting with the independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at the Institute for Advanced Study.
