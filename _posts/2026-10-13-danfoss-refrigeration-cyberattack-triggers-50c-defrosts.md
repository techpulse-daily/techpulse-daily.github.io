A cyberattack targeted Danfoss refrigeration control systems used by several European supermarket chains, causing automatic defrost cycles to run continuously at 50 °C. The incident also disrupted electronic shelf‑label pricing and online ordering systems at affected stores.

## Why it matters

The forced defrosting can spoil perishable goods, leading to significant financial losses for retailers. Compromised pricing displays and delivery platforms illustrate the broader risk of connected retail infrastructure. Engineers must ensure robust security and isolation for IoT devices that control critical physical processes.

## The key fact

The attack forced refrigeration units to run continuous defrosting at a temperature set to 50 °C, far above the normal two‑hour daily cycle.

## Context

The affected refrigeration controls are Danfoss‑manufactured units deployed in French supermarkets such as Intermarché, Leclerc, and Coopérative U, highlighting the vulnerability of widely used commercial IoT hardware.
