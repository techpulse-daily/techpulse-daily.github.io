An ex‑colleague called the author to ask for a tester recommendation, the author replied “How about me?”, met the startup CTO on Saturday and received a job offer confirmation on Monday.

## Why it matters

It demonstrates that personal referrals can bypass automated resume filters that many engineers face. It highlights the growing demand for QA expertise in AI‑focused products. It shows that hiring for AI testing roles may prioritize trust and judgment over specific tool checklists.

## The key fact

The author learned he passed the interview when his colleague said, “Wait for HR to contact you.”

## Context

After 15 years in QA, the author was applying cold to jobs with little response before the referral led to the new position at an AI agent web platform startup.
